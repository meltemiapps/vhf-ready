# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 19 November 2026_

The canonical version of this policy is at <https://meltemiapps.co.uk/vhf-ready/privacy/>.

VHF Ready is an unofficial study app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any training organisation, examining body, regulator or government agency (see [DISCLAIMER.md](DISCLAIMER.md)).

## Summary

VHF Ready does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

## What the app stores

The app saves your study progress on your device only, using standard iOS app storage:

- which lessons you have completed
- the last lesson you opened
- your best quiz scores
- which flashcards you have marked as known
- that you have accepted the in-app disclaimer

This data never leaves your device. It isn't sent to the developer or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data. You can also clear it at any time with **About & privacy › Reset progress**.

Anything you type into the practice Mayday builder exists only while that screen is open and is not saved.

## What the app does not do

- No accounts, sign-in or registration
- No analytics, crash reporting or usage tracking
- No advertising or advertising identifiers
- No third-party software development kits
- No network requests (links to this policy and the source code open in your browser only if you tap them)

## Read-aloud

The "Hear it read aloud" feature uses Apple's on-device speech synthesis. No audio or text is sent to the developer.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Open source

The full source code is public, so anyone can verify these statements.

Any change to this policy will be published in this file in the public repository, with a new "Last updated" date.
